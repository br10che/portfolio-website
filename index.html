/ for pharmacies

Your products deserve more than a name and a price. I get them ready to sell online.

Opening an e-shop, or listing on Wolt or another delivery app? Most pharmacy systems only hold a short product name, a barcode and a price. Customers online need much more before they buy. I prepare it for you, product by product, so your shelves look as good online as they do in the store.

Clear descriptions

What the product is, who it's for and how it helps, written so a customer understands it in a few seconds.

The right photos

Clean, matching photos for every product, checked against the barcode, so the picture always shows the right item, size and shade.

Ingredients & how to use

Full ingredient lists, active ingredients, how to use and warnings, taken from the brand's official information.

An organised catalogue

Correct names and sizes, no duplicates, clear categories and brands, so customers find what they're looking for.

what you get

  • Customers trust a page that answers their questions, so they buy more easily.
  • Fewer phone calls asking "what is this for?"
  • A better chance of being found on Google.
  • Files ready for your e-shop, your system or a delivery app.

how we work

  1. You send me your product listAn Excel export from your system is enough, even if the names are messy.
  2. I prepare the productsDescriptions, photos, ingredients and categories, checked one by one.
  3. You get them ready to useFiles ready for your e-shop, or I add them straight into your system.

I already do this for pharmacies in Cyprus: thousands of products prepared for their e-shops and delivery apps.

I work remotely, so it doesn't matter where your pharmacy is. I speak Greek, English and Russian.

/ about me

I'm a Computer Science student based in Limassol, Cyprus. My route into tech is a bit different: alongside my studies I've worked in pharmacy and e‑commerce operations, so I know how a business actually runs day to day, not just how to write code for it.

A lot of my work is product data: cleaning catalogs, matching barcodes, organising thousands of SKUs in spreadsheets and an ERP system. It taught me to care about accuracy, and about the small detail that quietly breaks everything if nobody checks it.

I also hold a diploma as a Computer and Network Technician, and I'm building on it with data work and cybersecurity. I speak English, Russian and Greek, which helps a lot in any role that talks to people.
